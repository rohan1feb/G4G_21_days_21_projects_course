Assignment: What does "packing = False" mean?

Solution:
This refers to the method the training dataset is treated. Each dataset text is treated as an independent sample. Multiple short examples are not merged to form long sentences.

Pros:
Easier to debug and interpret

Cons:
Less efficient if training dataset has a lot of short texts.
